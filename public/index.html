About Cyber Essentials

Five controls stop most common attacks

The UK government-backed scheme comes down to five things. We help you get each one right and keep the evidence ready.

Control 1

Firewalls

Only the traffic you mean to allow gets in.

Control 2

Secure configuration

Default passwords and unused features switched off.

Control 3

Security updates

High and critical patches applied within 14 days.

Control 4

User access control

People get the access they need and no more.

Control 5

Malware protection

Every device protected against known malicious software.
